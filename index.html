01
Cogknit · Helsinki
2025 – 2026

Strategic community building

Community strategy Engagement design Competitive research Ambassador model

Tasked with building Cogknit's community for internationally mobile talent in Finland from the ground up, I began by studying how high-performing communities operate — drawing on Duolingo's engagement mechanics, the 1990 niche community pyramid model, and the architecture of communities like Lovable. I then designed a layered community model: an open public square, career-stage pods for psychological safety, industry pods for peer exchange, and locked inner-circle pods earned through demonstrated engagement. I also conducted a structured competitive analysis, mapping Cogknit's positioning against existing platforms in Finland. At the end of the role, I produced a full strategic handover report — including a proposed ambassador model — to ensure the work could scale beyond a single coordinator.

02
Cogknit · Kyoto University · ICLS
2022 – 2026

Data-driven measurement & research design

KPI design Quantitative research Questionnaire design SPSS · R

My core research skill is making phenomena measurable with rigor. At Cogknit, I designed a KPI framework for community health — translating vague notions of "engagement" into specific, trackable metrics across five dimensions. Earlier, as a research assistant at Kyoto University, I designed quantitative questionnaires to measure the efficacy of safety training programs, conducting a pilot study with over 50 construction workers. That work was presented at EAPRIL and published in the proceedings of ICLS 2022. Across both contexts, the through-line is the same: building measurement tools that are valid, consistent, and actually reflect what matters.

Proactive, self-directed communication

Independent work Stakeholder management Cross-cultural coordination Notion

I consistently work in environments where structure is partial or absent — and I create it rather than waiting for it. As a research assistant, I organised school visits and coordinated communication between international researchers and local stakeholders, and delivered annual progress reports to company executives. In a startup context, I identified early that the team lacked a shared rhythm and proposed a weekly meeting structure to address it. I manage my workload in Notion, set my own deadlines, and navigate ambiguity by asking clear questions of myself first. The Cogknit end-of-role report — produced independently and covering strategy, engagement, governance, and forward recommendations — is itself a product of this working style.
